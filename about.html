The AcroVault Mission

In the modern threat landscape, security professionals waste countless hours hunting for functional, sandboxed, and reliable intelligence tools. The internet is littered with dead links, unmaintained GitHub repositories, and Paywalled services that gatekeep essential security functions.

Our Objective

To provide an unparalleled, frictionless, and comprehensive directory of client-side cyber tools and open-source intelligence interfaces. Zero installation. Zero telemetry. Instant execution.

Why Client-Side?

Security requires trust. By hosting the vast majority of our tools as native client-side web applications, we ensure that your sensitive queries—whether hashing a malicious payload or analyzing a forensic artifact—never leave your browser sandbox. We don't want your data, and our architecture proves it.
