1. Introduction and Scope

Welcome to AcroVault. At AcroVault ("we," "us," or "our"), we understand that in the cybersecurity industry, privacy and data protection are not just regulatory requirements—they are the foundation of our business. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, use our SaaS platform, and interact with our services (collectively, the "Services").

2. Information We Collect

To provide our enterprise-grade security services, we collect the following types of information:

  • Account and Contact Information: Name, email address, company name, phone number, and billing details provided during registration.
  • System and Usage Data: IP addresses, browser types, operating systems, and logs of your interactions with our platform.
  • Security Telemetry Data: For AcroVault to function, we analyze metadata, network traffic patterns, and security event logs from your connected infrastructure. Note: We do not inspect the payload of your encrypted data unless explicitly configured by you for threat analysis.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information strictly for the following purposes:

  • Service Provisioning: To operate, maintain, and deliver the AcroVault cybersecurity services.
  • Threat Detection and Analytics: To identify anomalies, prevent cyberattacks, and improve our proprietary threat intelligence algorithms.
  • Communication: To send administrative notices, security alerts, product updates, and customer support responses.
  • Compliance: To fulfill legal obligations and enforce our Terms of Service.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors (e.g., cloud hosting providers, payment processors) who operate under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Requirements: If required to do so by law, or in response to valid requests by public authorities (e.g., a court or government agency).
  • Business Transfers: In connection with any merger, sale of company assets, or acquisition of all or a portion of our business.

5. Data Security and Retention

We implement industry-leading technical and organizational measures to secure your data, including end-to-end encryption, regular penetration testing, and strict access controls. We retain your personal and telemetry data only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy or as required by law. Upon account termination, your data is securely wiped from our primary systems within 30 days.

6. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g., GDPR, CCPA), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. To exercise these rights or to contact our Data Protection Officer, please email us at privacy@acrovault.com.
