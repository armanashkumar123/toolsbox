💡 Power of Compounding & 15x15x15 Rule

The 15x15x15 rule is a benchmark in Indian mutual funds: Investing ₹15,000/month for 15 years at an expected return rate of 15% p.a. creates a corpus of over ₹1 Crore! Notice how your returns surpass your invested capital in later years due to exponential compounding.

⚖️ SIP vs Lumpsum: Which is better?

  • SIP (Systematic Investment Plan): Ideal for salaried investors. Benefits from Rupee Cost Averaging, eliminating market timing risks.
  • Lumpsum: Great when you have a surplus lump sum amount (e.g. bonus, property sale). Works best during market corrections or for long horizons.

🏛️ Mutual Fund Tax Structure in India

Understanding taxation on Equity Mutual Funds (as per recent budget guidelines):

  • LTCG (Long-Term Capital Gains): Gains beyond ₹1.25 Lakh per financial year are taxed at 12.5% without indexation.
  • STCG (Short-Term Capital Gains): Investments held under 1 year are taxed at 20%.
