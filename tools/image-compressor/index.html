Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data and images safe?
Yes, absolutely! All image compression, resizing, and format conversions are performed 100% locally on your device using JavaScript and the HTML5 Canvas API. No images leave your device.
Why convert images to WebP format?
WebP is a modern image format that provides superior lossy and lossless compression for web images. WebP images are typically 30% to 50% smaller than JPEGs at similar quality levels, leading to faster website load times.
How does the Quality slider work?
The quality slider adjusts the compression level applied to lossy formats like WebP and JPEG. Higher percentages retain more detail and fidelity, while lower values reduce file size more aggressively.
Can I resize image dimensions at the same time?
Yes! You can scale images down by a percentage (e.g. 75%, 50%) or specify custom maximum width and height in pixels while maintaining the aspect ratio automatically.
