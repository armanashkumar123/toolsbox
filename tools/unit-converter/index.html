Frequently Asked Questions

How accurate are these unit conversions?
All mathematical factors conform to international standard definitions (ISO, NIST, BIPM). Our tool uses double-precision floating-point arithmetic with configurable precision up to 8 decimal places or scientific notation.
What is the difference between Binary and Decimal Data storage units?
Binary data units (traditional IEC standard) use powers of 2 (\(1\text{ KB} = 1024\text{ Bytes}\)), while Decimal data storage (SI standard used by hard drive makers) uses powers of 10 (\(1\text{ KB} = 1000\text{ Bytes}\)). You can toggle between both standards in the toolbar.
Why does Temperature conversion require special formulas?
Unlike length or weight, temperature scales have different zero reference points. For instance, \(0^\circ\text{C} = 32^\circ\text{F} = 273.15\text{ K}\). Thus, temperature conversion requires offset calculations in addition to scaling factors.
